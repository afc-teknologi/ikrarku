# ikrarku Sites CMS v0.10

## Included improvements

- Database-backed inbound chat for public visitors and authenticated Users.
- Outbound User messaging for Admin, Editor, and Customer Service.
- Centralized Template Approval under Tasks with Approve, Reject, feedback, and email notification.
- Template status workflow: Pending, Publish, Reject.
- Dedicated Site Pages: Pages, List Undangan, and RSVP Confirmation.
- Drag-and-drop Canvas ordering in Page Structure and the Canvas workspace.
- Public wedding URL at `http://localhost:5173/<slug>`.
- Editable Buka Undangan labels, text, button, typography, color, alignment, padding, and margin.
- Fixed Use Template workflow that adds new Canvas content and opens the editor.
- Full-width featured Article banner plus database-driven cards on the landing page.
- Custom role permissions for Template Approval and outbound User messaging.
